# Local Graph service and failure containment

AB-START-001; Design v0.2; reversible implementation decisions.

Service owned/retained Graph workers and newly queued local capture clocks between ready IPC clients. Leave idle all-run history reconciliation to the unchanged full outer pass. These local scheduling hints carry no admission authority; starts and settlements keep fresh Grant/input/budget checks. This avoids introducing a cross-request business-proof cache or a second scheduler.

Represent a confirmed retained-overrun with a private non-deserializable single-use typed result. A separate fresh transaction rechecks the stopped failure and exact original facts before blocking the Factory target. Do not catch an arbitrary RESOURCE_LIMIT or turn an overrun into success, a refund, a retry or a stopped-process receipt.

Treat an already-bound, exact double-Z MCP not yet adopted by the test controller as unfinished observation for that discovery. Keep prior history, omit the fresh row and require the original final actual wait/gone checks. Zombie is not reaped. All other identity/argv failures remain refusals.

Actual source-attributed evidence and limitations are in CONTAINMENT_WAKE_AND_BROWSER_RESULTS.json. The local successful Owner assertion does not prove a controlled speedup, a unique CI root cause or a Mac fix; the exact candidate must still pass remote gates.
