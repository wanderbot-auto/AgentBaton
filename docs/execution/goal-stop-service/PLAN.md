## ADR

The exact8c292 Intel projection has producer4005 against original4000, and unstarted capture with4 null worker metrics; actual4362 includes357 additional queue service. It is not the prior030 completed-thread join gap and not proof of CPU or a particular read phase. Source establishes a service opportunity: after Supervisor commits a correlated terminal Goal Turn during a ready batch, only worker/data services follow; the original full Goal stop sweep otherwise waits for outer tick_goals. The duration still runs while the real Provider waits for that stop. The real magnitude of this interval was not sampled.

Extract the original complete `goal_stop_requests → supervisor.cancel` loop verbatim into private stop-only service. Keep the original outer unconditional fresh sweep and full later Goal selection/admission exactly where they were. A Supervisor-owned worker's terminal4-state outcome may set a coalesced process-local boolean only after its turn transaction commits and existing Goal session attribution succeeds. The signal is not Deserialize, permission, a live-scope cache or a stop/lease/capture receipt. Missing hints retain old outer behavior; failed commit/attribution or fresh stop proof propagates failure and preserves existing owned fatal cleanup.

The ready batch consumes this signal immediately after a successful Supervisor.tick and before its original worker services. Without a hint it does not perform another graph sweep. The hinted path runs every original current Task/lineage/Factory-policy/node/session/job/terminal-turn stop predicate; it never invokes full tick_goals, new admission, a launch, a retry or a new epoch. The full sweep may also service other already-authorized restrictive stop requests, as before; the signal itself selects no recipient or authority. Only an eventual actual WorkerReceipt can confirm process reap/release the original lease or queue capture.

Original global Graph reconcile→prepare/spawn/capture order stays intact. This proposal only moves an existing restrictive stop-service opportunity; it does not use withdrawn a067 early-spawn ordering, omit proof, shorten charged clocks, refund queue time, increase original4s/20s/30s/55s/60s, or claim the Intel cause has been resolved.
